# MLR_Models_TPAI_2025
Literature for multi-vocal literature review of models for testing process assessment and improvement
This source includes a single excel workbook with 2 sheets. 
**Sheet 1: "Literature_References**
This sheet includes list of literature that was found as a result of search string. Overall 907 studies were found out of 818 studies were exluded as per inclusion/exclusion criteria on the basis of Year, Language, Availability and Content. Remaining studies were filtered to check duplications. Out of 89, 72 uniques studies were left. The Citations of these studies are provided here.
**Sheet 2: "Model_Definitions**
In order to determine the quality of testing models found, additional search was performed to find studies providing definition or structure of the models. This sheet contains a list of references to those models. For testing models for which a separate study was not found, but there were other studies referring to those models, are also lsited here, to support the data on these models. 
**END**
